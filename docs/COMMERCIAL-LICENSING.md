# Commercial licensing and reserved ViTech rights

This policy records the project owner's direction to preserve ViTech's commercial options. It explains the public/commercial boundary; it does not amend MPL-2.0 or Apache-2.0, establish ownership of third-party work, or activate the proposed CLA.

## Public permissions and obligations

The per-path grants in `LICENSE` and `REUSE.toml` govern this release. MPL-2.0 covers the kernel, SDK, reference CLI, Proof Pack and RTOS enforcement implementation. Apache-2.0 covers the mapped protocol, documentation and governance material. Both permit commercial use subject to their terms. Compliant use, modification, redistribution and factual reference do not require an additional ViTech approval.

Preserve applicable copyright, licence and attribution notices. Distribution of MPL-covered source and modifications must retain the MPL rights; executable distribution must meet its source-availability obligations. Apache redistribution must meet its licence, modification-notice and applicable NOTICE requirements. A separate-file larger work may have other terms where the relevant licence permits. The licence texts control if this explanation differs from them.

## Rights requiring a separate agreement

Obtain a written agreement from an authorized representative of the verified ViTech rightsholder before relying on any of these additional rights:

| Requested right | Required approval and scope |
| --- | --- |
| A ViTech-issued alternative licence that waives obligations otherwise applicable to ViTech-controlled code | Specific version/files, rights, fees and terms agreed in writing; sufficient rights from all affected contributors must be verified first |
| Access to or use of proprietary ViTech code, protected Capsule content or private enterprise services excluded from this repository | Separate access and licensing contract from the relevant rightsholder; this repository grants no rights in excluded material |
| Official ViTech endorsement, certification, affiliation or branding beyond permissions provided by law or an existing agreement | Express written authorization for that representation or brand use; factual attribution is not endorsement |
| Support, warranties, indemnities or service commitments supplied by ViTech | Separate contract defining the actual supplier and obligations; third parties cannot bind ViTech |

Start an inquiry through the maintainer contact route in `SUPPORT.md`. Do not publish confidential proposals, contributor signatures, registration records or personal data in an issue. A request, repository label, checkbox, ordinary PR, bot response or silence does not constitute the additional agreement. No commercial offer or authority to sign for an unresolved legal entity is established here.

## Future licensing and contribution intake

ViTech's policy is to retain the option to offer different terms for material it owns or has sufficient permission to relicense. No future proprietary grant is promised for third-party contributions until the required rights have been obtained. Existing valid MPL-2.0 and Apache-2.0 grants remain available for versions already distributed under them.

Before accepting contributions intended for alternative licensing, complete the decisions in [Legal activation decisions](engineering/LEGAL-ACTIVATION-DECISIONS.md): receiving entity and capacity, approved versioned agreement, patent and sublicensing terms, employer/co-owner permissions, scope of historical and pending contributions, attributable acceptance and private evidence custody. The CLA in PR #12 remains **DRAFT / NOT EFFECTIVE**; no signed acceptance is claimed. Repository admission controls and legal assent are separate.

Copyright notices and contributor agreements protect rights within their actual scope. This policy does not claim exclusive ownership of abstract ideas, general system logic, interoperability concepts or every independent implementation. Patent, confidentiality and trademark questions require assessment of the actual rights and applicable law; this policy neither invents new rights nor withdraws licence grants already made.

## Release decision

For each package, retain its source identities, licence map, third-party notices/SBOMs and a documented rights decision for the intended distribution. A green test proves neither legal title nor permission to remove copyleft obligations. Developer-preview publication, CLA activation and any alternative commercial licence are distinct decisions.
